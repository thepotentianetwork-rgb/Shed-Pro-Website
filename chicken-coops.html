Construction

The same build as a ShedPro shed

Every coop is built by the crew that builds our sheds, with the same materials.

  • 2×4 framing, 16" on center. Walls are framed the way we frame our sheds.
  • The same siding as our sheds. LP SmartSide engineered wood siding, painted in your choice of colors.
  • The same trim and roofing. Painted trim, architectural shingles as standard, and metal roofing as an upgrade.
  • Built for chickens. An outside nesting box with a hinged lid, a standard grid window, and on Coop + Run models a chicken door and ramp into the run.

Service Area

Chicken coops in our four main build areas

We’re based at 2645 S Highway 89 in Wellsville. A travel fee applies to every build. Permits and setbacks are your responsibility — we recommend you pull the permit yourself, and we build to code.

Cache County

Home base is Wellsville, in Cache Valley, so Logan, Nibley, Hyrum, and the rest of the county are close to the shop. If you’re putting a coop on a city lot in Logan, confirm flock limits, setbacks, and whether you need a permit with Logan Community Development or Cache County before you order.

Box Elder County

Brigham City, Tremonton, and unincorporated Box Elder are a short trip from Wellsville. Larger lots here often have room for a Coop + Run. Check with Brigham City or the Box Elder County Building Department for accessory-structure rules on your address.

Salt Lake County

We build chicken coops in Salt Lake County, including the Salt Lake City area. A raised coop is often the better fit on a smaller backyard. Confirm setbacks, flock limits, and permit rules with your city or the county before you finalize a size.

Utah County / Provo

We build in the Provo / Utah County area as well. Design the coop in 3D, request a quote with your ZIP, and we’ll include the travel fee for your address. Check with Provo or your Utah County city for chicken-keeping and accessory-structure rules.

FAQ

Questions about ShedPro coops

Where does ShedPro build chicken coops?

Most of our coop builds are in Box Elder County, Cache County, Salt Lake County, and the Provo / Utah County area. We’re based at 2645 S Highway 89 in Wellsville, Utah. A travel fee applies to every build.

How much does a ShedPro chicken coop cost?

Starting prices for the base build are $2,500 for a 4×5 raised coop, $2,900 for a 4×7, $3,500 for a 6×7, $4,300 for a 6×12 Coop + Run, $4,700 for a 6×14, and $5,200 for a 6×16. Upgrades and site prep are priced in the 3D designer. A travel fee applies to every build.

How long does a chicken coop build take?

Builds take 1–3 days once we schedule yours with you.

What is the deposit, and how do I pay?

A 30% deposit is required. You get 3% off for cash, check, or ACH. We do not offer financing.

Do I need a permit for a chicken coop?

Permits and setbacks are your responsibility. We recommend that you pull the permit yourself, and we build to code. Rules vary by city and county, so confirm with your planning or building office before you order.

Do you offer gravel prep?

Yes. Gravel prep starts at $500 for raised coops and $1,000 for Coop + Run builds, and leveling is included. Your site and access can change the final price, and we confirm it with you before you order.

Can I design a chicken coop in 3D?

Yes. Open the ShedPro coop designer to pick a raised coop or a coop + run, choose the size, colors, roof, windows, and upgrades, and see the price as you go. Then request a quote.
