What You Can Design

Serious depth, for custom work

The same level of detail we use to plan a build. Change anything and the 3D model updates as you go, so what you submit is the shed you actually want.

6 styles & starter builds

Gable / A-Frame (our most popular), Barn, Lean-To, Poolhouse (hip roof), 3 Peak and 4 Peak. Start from a fully loaded starter build or design from scratch.

Exact size & shape

Widths 6–26 ft and lengths 6–34 ft in 2-foot steps, walls 6–12 ft, roof pitch from 6/12 to 12/12, and dormers on gable styles.

Siding, trim & real paint codes

Vertical, horizontal lap, board & batten or 1x6 pine tongue-and-groove with stain. Pick schemes and swatches, or search real Sherwin-Williams, Behr, Benjamin Moore, Valspar, PPG/Glidden and Dunn-Edwards colors by code.

Roof

Architectural shingles or metal in multiple colors, ridge vent, gable vents and a cupola.

Doors & windows you place

Single, double, residential, sliding glass, fairytale and roll-up garage doors, windows and transoms. Slide each one to the exact spot on the wall, then add grids, shutters, flower boxes, a door overhang or a skylight.

Porch or awning

A covered front or side porch built into the shed’s size, or an awning added outside it, for a cabin look.

Interior you can walk into

Look around inside in 3D. Choose bare studs, drywall, or drywall and paint, flooring such as luxury vinyl plank, lofts, shelves you drag into place, and insulation options including spray foam.

Power-ready electrical

Power-ready electrical packages with porch lights you position on the wall, plus a day and night view to see how it looks lit up.

Foundation, access & site

Gravel pad, concrete pad poured by us or an existing foundation, framing upgrades, ramps, stairs and ladders, and site prep such as removing an old shed or moving sprinkler heads.

3D Shed Designer FAQ

Common questions

Is the ShedPro 3D shed designer really free?

Yes. It's free to use in any web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with no account or download. Your design saves automatically on your device, and you can share it with a link. Submitting a design for an estimate is free and there's no obligation to buy.

Will ShedPro build exactly what I design?

Yes, that's the point of the designer. Most of our sheds start with a customer designing it themselves and submitting it. We review your design, confirm the details with you (size, site, doors, windows, colors), send your estimate, and build the shed you designed. If something in your design won't work on your site, we'll tell you before you commit.

Does the designer show a price?

The designer is for getting your shed exactly how you want it. When you submit your design, a ShedPro rep reviews it and calls you within 24 hours, then sends your estimate. Your estimate is itemized to match the design you sent.

What can I customize in the 3D designer?

Almost everything we build. Style, exact size, wall height and roof pitch; dormers on gable styles; siding type (vertical, horizontal lap, board and batten or pine tongue-and-groove) with colors or real brand paint codes; shingle or metal roof, vents and cupola; doors, windows and transoms you slide into position, with grids, shutters and flower boxes; a porch or awning; interior finish, flooring, lofts, shelves and insulation; power-ready electrical and porch lights; foundation, ramps and site prep. You can look at it from every angle, inside and out.

What shed styles and sizes can I design?

Gable / A-Frame (one style, our most popular, with an optional covered awning or porch for a cabin look), Barn, Lean-To, Poolhouse, 3 Peak and 4 Peak. Widths run 6 to 26 ft and lengths 6 to 34 ft in 2-foot steps, with 6 to 12 ft walls. Popular sizes include 8×10, 8×12, 10×12, 10×16, 12×16, 12×20, 12×24 and 16×24.

Is this just a basic shed template or kit configurator?

No. It was built for custom work. Instead of picking from a few fixed kits, you set the dimensions, roof pitch, materials and colors yourself and place each door, window, light and shelf where you want it. Most of the sheds we build start as a customer's own design in this tool.

What's the difference between an awning and a porch?

An awning is added outside your shed and doesn't count toward its square footage: a 12×16 with a 4 ft awning is a full 12×16 enclosed shed plus the awning. A porch is built inside the shed's size: a 12×16 with a 4 ft front porch is 12×16 overall with 12×12 enclosed.

Our own crew builds every shed on your property. We've built 1,000+ sheds, with 10+ years of hands-on experience behind every build. Most sheds are finished in 1 to 3 days.
