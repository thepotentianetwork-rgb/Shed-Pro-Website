Top 5 Ways Homeowners Use Their Custom Sheds

June 2025 • 5 min read • By ShedPro LLC

A custom shed is one of the most versatile investments a homeowner can make. Unlike pre-fab kits from the hardware store, a shed built on-site by ShedPro LLC is designed around how you actually live — your space, your needs, your vision. Over the years we've built hundreds of sheds across Utah and Idaho, and we've noticed that most homeowners fall into one of five categories when it comes to how they use their shed. Here they are.

1

Storage — The Classic

It's the most common reason homeowners build a shed, and for good reason. Whether it's lawn equipment, holiday decorations, sports gear, tools, or seasonal items taking over your garage — a dedicated storage shed gives everything a home. The key is building it big enough. We hear it all the time: "I wish I'd gone bigger." Our advice? Size up at least one step from what you think you need. A 12x16 feels huge until you start filling it. We can help you plan the right layout during a free in-home consultation.

2

Workshop — The Serious Space

Woodworkers, mechanics, welders, hobbyists — if you do any kind of hands-on work, a dedicated workshop shed changes everything. No more working in the driveway, setting up and tearing down every time, or fighting for space in the garage. A workshop shed gives you a permanent, purpose-built space that's always ready. We build workshop sheds with power-ready electrical packages — so you have outlets, lighting, and power exactly where you need it. Most serious workshops go with our Core or Essential electrical tier to handle power tools and compressors.

3

She-Shed / Retreat — The Personal Escape

One of the fastest-growing trends we're seeing in Utah and Idaho. A she-shed (or he-shed, or just "the retreat") is a private space that belongs to you — a reading room, art studio, meditation space, home office, or just somewhere to decompress away from the house. These builds tend to go heavy on windows for natural light, good electrical for outlets and lighting, and sometimes even mini-split AC units. The beauty of building custom is that you get exactly the vibe you're going for — right down to the Sherwin-Williams color on the walls.

4

Home Office — The Work-From-Home Game Changer

Since 2020, backyard office sheds have exploded in demand — and it's easy to see why. Working from home is great until your kitchen table becomes your desk, your kids are in the background of every call, and you never truly "leave" work. A dedicated backyard office solves all of that. It's a separate space with a door you can close, proper lighting, outlets for all your equipment, and a commute that's 30 seconds. We've built dozens of home office sheds across the Wasatch Front — typically with large windows, full electrical, and a clean modern finish.

5

Pool House or Garden Shed — The Outdoor Extension

For homeowners with pools, large gardens, or entertaining spaces, a pool house or garden shed is a natural extension of the backyard. Pool houses give you a place to store floats, chemicals, and towels — or even add a bathroom and changing area. Garden sheds keep your tools organized and your soil, seeds, and supplies dry and accessible. We've built beautiful pool houses with French doors, large windows, and metal roofing that become showpieces of the backyard. If you're building one, come in for a free consultation — we'll walk your property and help you figure out the perfect placement and size.

What's Most Popular?

Based on our builds across Utah and Idaho, the A-Frame / Gable is our #1 most requested style — its classic peaked roofline handles Utah snow loads perfectly and works in any backyard. Close behind is the Barn Style, which gives you dramatically more vertical space for workshops or serious storage without increasing your footprint. Both styles work beautifully for any of the five uses above.

What About You?

Most homeowners have one primary use in mind when they start — but once they have the space, they find themselves using it for two or three things. That's the beauty of building custom. We'll help you think through your layout, size, electrical needs, and features so the shed works for your life — not just one use case.

Not sure which type is right for you? That's exactly what our free in-home consultation is for. We'll come to your property, walk the space with you, and help you figure out what makes the most sense for your yard, your budget, and your goals.
